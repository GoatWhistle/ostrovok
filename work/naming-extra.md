## Notes

- Strongest in this batch: Isoline and Keystay (4). Isoline maps directly to the mechanism (walk-time isochrones to the sea) but is a technical word; Keystay is clean but generic and reads as a lock/key product in hotel tech.
- Drop: Horizon and Meridian (1). Both are saturated hotel names in Russia and internationally, Le Méridien is a Marriott brand.
- Contour is a hard no for the RU market: СКБ Контур already sells Контур.Отель to hoteliers, the same audience and category.
- Benchmark and Shoreline collide with real hotel operators; Shoreline also narrows the product to the sea.
- Topo, Fieldnote, Truenorth are mid: no direct hotel booking collision, but weak or awkward in Russian.
- Not checked: trademarks in classes 39/43, domains, app-store names, Telegram/VK handles.
